Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2026

Overview

Tayyari helps users plan travel with reminders and packing assistance. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about data usage.

What data we store

  • By default, trip data, holiday schedules, reminders, and packing lists are stored locally on your device.
  • Using an account for cloud sync is optional and not required to use core app features.

Optional sign-in and sync

If you choose to sign in, selected app data may be synced securely to enable cross-device access. If you do not sign in, your data remains on-device only.

Notifications

Tayyari uses local notifications on your device to remind you about booking windows and travel tasks.

Third-party services

Tayyari may use third-party services for authentication or optional sync. These services process data according to their own privacy terms.

Your choices

  • You can use Tayyari without creating an account.
  • You can disable notifications in device settings.
  • You can remove app data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@tayyari.app
