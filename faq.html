FAQ

Does Tayyari book IRCTC tickets?

No. It reminds you the morning booking opens (120 days before the journey) and lists curated direct trains. You book on IRCTC.

Are train times live?

No. Hours are approximate popular services. Check IRCTC before you travel.

Which cities are covered?

Pune, Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Goa, Kochi, Lucknow, and Ujjain.

What counts as a long weekend?

A Friday or Monday holiday needs no extra leave. A Thursday or Tuesday holiday needs one bridge day.
