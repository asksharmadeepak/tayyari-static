What Tayyari does

Long weekends

Friday and Monday holidays are ready-made. Thursday and Tuesday need one extra leave day. 2026 and 2027 are listed with the morning IRCTC opens.

Direct trains

Curated trains from your city, with approximate hours. Not live seats. Start with Pune to Goa, Pune to Ujjain, or Mumbai to Goa.

A plan and a packing list

Beach weekends are not temple weekends. Goa gets cafés. Ujjain gets Mahakaleshwar.
