About Tayyari

Tayyari lists the long weekends still ahead, curated direct trains from your city, a short plan for the destination, and a reminder the morning IRCTC booking opens. It does not sell tickets and it does not show live berth availability.

Tayyari is a D labs app. D labs also makes Homework Buddy, NextBinge, and MyBinge.
