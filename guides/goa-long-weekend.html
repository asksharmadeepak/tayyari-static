Goa long weekend

Beaches, cafés, and an easy Konkan pace. Pack for a beach weekend, not a generic city break.

Two or three days

Day 1

  • Settle near Calangute, Baga, or Anjuna
  • Sunset walk on the beach
  • Dinner at a beach shack

Day 2

  • Morning swim
  • Café crawl in Anjuna or Assagao
  • Fort Aguada or Chapora for views

Day 3

  • Slow brunch by the sea
  • Spices or cashews before the return train

Packing

  • Swimwear
  • Sunscreen SPF 50+
  • Flip-flops
  • Hat
  • Light cotton clothes

Direct trains into Goa

  • Delhi to Goa · Goa Sampark Kranti ~27h
  • Mumbai to Goa · Mandovi Express ~9h
  • Bangalore to Goa · Vasco Express ~12h
  • Hyderabad to Goa · Vasco Express ~16h
  • Chennai to Goa · Chennai Vasco Express ~22h
  • Pune to Goa · Goa Express ~11h
  • Ahmedabad to Goa · Ahmedabad Madgaon SF ~18h
