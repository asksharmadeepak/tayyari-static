Ujjain and Mahakaleshwar

Mahakaleshwar and the sacred heart of Malwa. Pack for a temple weekend, not a generic city break.

Two or three days

Day 1

  • Mahakaleshwar darshan — check Bhasma Aarti slots early
  • Evening aarti at Ram Ghat
  • Simple dinner near the temple

Day 2

  • Kal Bhairav in the morning
  • Harsiddhi Temple
  • Old-city sweet shops

Day 3

  • Sandipani Ashram or Vedh Shala if you have time
  • Prasad, then the return train

Packing

  • Modest clothing
  • Dupatta or scarf
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Socks for shoe removal
  • Small bag for offerings

Direct trains into Ujjain

  • Delhi to Ujjain · Malwa Express ~12h
  • Mumbai to Ujjain · Avantika Express ~12h
  • Pune to Ujjain · Pune Indore SF (via UJN) ~12h
