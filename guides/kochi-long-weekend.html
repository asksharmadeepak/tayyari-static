Kochi long weekend

Harbour breezes, spice lanes, and Fort Kochi. Pack for a city weekend, not a generic city break.

Two or three days

Day 1

  • Fort Kochi and the Chinese fishing nets
  • Café in Jew Town
  • Sunset by the harbour

Day 2

  • Dutch Palace and the spice market
  • Short ferry if the weather holds
  • Seafood dinner in Fort Kochi

Day 3

  • Kerala breakfast
  • Spices or coir before departure

Packing

  • Light cotton clothes
  • Mosquito repellent
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Light rain jacket in monsoon
  • Cap

Direct trains into Kochi

  • Delhi to Kochi · Kerela Sampark Kranti ~42h
  • Mumbai to Kochi · Netravati Express ~24h
  • Bangalore to Kochi · Island Express ~12h
  • Chennai to Kochi · Alleppey Express ~11h
